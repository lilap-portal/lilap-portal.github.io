UX Audit · Chapter 01

A working diagnosis of the B2B wholesale portal

An AI-assisted UX evaluation of Lilla P's B2B Portal test instance — guided by a UX Assessment Lead, covering heuristic evaluation, key-journey walkthroughs, and responsive/accessibility spot checks.

About this audit

The B2B Portal is already central to the daily work of Sales Reps, wholesale buyers, Sales Managers, and admin users. Before introducing new functionality — and eventually building the broader Product Platform — this audit builds a clear picture of how the current experience performs: where it creates friction, which tasks require unnecessary effort, and what is already working well and should be preserved.

The evaluation uses AI tools, guided throughout by a UX Assessment Lead who defines scope, directs each case, and validates every finding, while AI accelerates navigation, evidence capture, comparison, and synthesis.

Area 1
Targeted heuristic evaluation

Reviewed against a focused set of Nielsen's heuristics: consistency and standards, visibility of system status, error prevention, and error recovery.

Area 2
Key journeys & cognitive walkthrough

A compact experience map of key journeys grouped by who performs them: Common, Buyer, Sales Rep, and Admin.

Area 3
Responsive & accessibility spot checks

A targeted look at representative screen sizes and high-risk interface patterns — not a full responsive or WCAG conformance audit.

Testing boundary: no financial transaction is ever completed, and "Submit Orders" is never clicked without explicit, case-by-case approval.
